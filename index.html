ASCII NIGHTS

Two doors, one flashlight, one mask, one fan and a battery that never lasts. Twenty-nine of them are out there tonight, and every one of them plays by its own rules.

Turn sound on: most of them warn you with a sound first.

A fan game. Not affiliated with Scott Cawthon or Five Nights at Freddy's.

Before your shift

CUSTOM NIGHT

Set each one from level 0 to 20. Level 0 switches it off for the night; at 20 it acts at every chance it gets and gives you the least time to react. Or pick a ready-made challenge.

HOW TO PLAY

    
    
      
  • Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM. Each hour lasts 30 real seconds.
    • 
      
  • The monitor, each closed door, the flashlight and the fan each use the same amount of power. The mask is free. If the power runs out, everything shuts off and something finds you in the dark.
    • 
      
  • The flashlight lights up the area around your mouse pointer: point at something, then hold F or the right mouse button. Whatever is in the light gets flashed. You can also hold the Flashlight button: the light stays where you last pointed in the office.
    • 
      
  • Freddy laughs every time he moves, but you never see him on the cameras. After the third laugh he's at the right door: shut it.
    • 
      
  • Bonnie walks from the stage to either door. Follow him on the cameras and shut the door when he's in the doorway; he goes straight back to the stage.
    • 
      
  • Chica walks from Backstage (CAM 3) through the Dining area to the West hall, slowly and without a sound. Check on her and click her on her camera to send her back a room. Once she's in the West hall, her next move gets you.
    • 
      
  • Foxy gets ready in Pirate Cove (CAM 4), then runs down the West hall to the left door. He can't get ready while you're on the cameras (any camera), so checking them now and then holds him back. When you hear him running, shut the left door: he slams into it, which knocks 5% off your power, and goes back to the cove.
    • 
      
  • Golden Freddy can be sitting in your office when you flip the monitor down. Tap the mask on, quickly.
    • 
      
  • Toy Bonnie, Toy Chica and Nedd Bear crawl through the ducts (the DUCTS tab on the monitor) toward the vent into your office; every move is a clank, louder the closer they are. Click a duct room to play the audio lure there: it pulls them, but not every time (Toy Chica follows it easily, Nedd Bear sometimes, Toy Bonnie hardly ever). When it gets dire, turn on the heater (Q): it pushes everyone back a room at a time, but uses power and heats the office. Toy Bonnie at the vent (a loud bang): put the mask on. Toy Chica or Nedd Bear at the vent: get them out fast, or they get you.
    • 
      
  • Withered Bonnie turns up on a timer. The monitor doesn't stop the timer, but he only appears once you flip the monitor down. Put the mask on and keep it on for 2 seconds.
    • 
      
  • Withered Foxy turns up in the West hall (CAM 5) now and then and charges the left door, with heavier steps than Foxy's. Shut the door on him. When you hear him stomp (the Left door button says OPEN IT), open the door, point at him in the doorway and shine the flashlight on him until he leaves (he goes pale while it works, then a ding). No need to shut the door again. Open it before the stomp and he lunges again!
    • 
      
  • The Puppet waits in the music box on CAM 7. One tap of Wind (or W) while you watch it winds it all the way up, however low it was. A ♪! on the Camera button means it's getting low.
    • 
      
  • Shadow Bonnie now and then makes the office and the cameras go dark. Only the flashlight shows what's there, and while it's dark it works on the cameras too.
    • 
      
  • Phantom Chica can show up when you flip the monitor down. Find and click her shadow cupcakes {^} (1 to 3) somewhere in the office. Miss them and she jumpscares you and takes 10% of your power.
    • 
      
  • Nightmare Freddy's Freddles pile up on your desk. Shine the flashlight on each one to send it away. If all three sit there too long, he comes, and he never leaves: put the mask on and keep it on for the rest of the night. Taking it off is deadly, and with the mask on you can't use the cameras, so keep those Freddles off the desk!
    • 
      
  • Nightmare Foxy makes no sound: the only way to see him coming is on the cameras (the Dining area, then the West or East hall). Shut that door: once he reaches it he waits a few seconds (unseen) before coming in, so there's still time. If he gets in, he sits in your office and never leaves, creeping closer bit by bit: keep pushing him back with the flashlight.
    • 
      
  • Nightmare Chica makes a strange noise and peers into your office from the top, then hides her cupcake {w} on one of the cameras. She stays until you find it. It's small and dull and blends into the picture, so look closely, and click it before time runs out.
    • 
      
  • Plushtrap giggles and hides somewhere in your office. Get the flashlight on him fast: he's quick to get you, but any light sends him away at once.
    • 
      
  • Chica's Magic Rainbow appears with a sparkle, over the office or the cameras, and chases your mouse pointer, a little slower than you can move it. Keep the mouse moving away from her for a few seconds until she gives up. If she catches the pointer, you're done.
    • 
      
  • Dee Dee may show up at the start of the night: she takes one random character away for the night, but everyone else gets 5 levels harder and coins turn up less often.
    • 
      
  • Funtime Freddy plays a little tune and waits in the West or East hall. Look at him on the cameras first (anything you do before looking doesn't count). If BonBon is on his hand, shut the door on his side. If not, she's at the other door: shine the flashlight there.
    • 
      
  • Funtime Foxy puts a SHOWTIME countdown above your monitor. When it reaches 0 the left door had better be shut. There's no other warning.
    • 
      
  • Bonnet runs across your office floor. Boop her nose (click the *) before she reaches the other side. She dodges the mouse, but you're faster: come at her from the front.
    • 
      
  • Twisted Foxy makes gravelly noises while he digs up out of the floor in the Prize Corner (CAM 7). Click him there again and again until he sinks back down.
    • 
      
  • Rockstar Bonnie lost his guitar. Buy it back in the SHOP before 6 AM (the higher his level, the pricier), or he comes for it at 5:59.
    • 
      
  • Nedd Bear crawls through the ducts with the toys (see Toy Bonnie and Toy Chica above).
    • 
      
  • The Flamethrower endo heats up your office fast. While he's there the fan can't cool it, only slow the heat down, until he leaves.
    • 
      
  • Toxic Springtrap fills the office with purple fumes and a TOXINS meter starts rising. Turn the fan on and keep both doors open to clear the air before it fills.
    • 
      
  • The Nightmare staff bot sits on your desk. When it beeps and blinks, its arrow points at a door: shut that one. If that door was already shut, it waits until you open it, and then you have to shut it again.
    • 
      
  • White Tiger doesn't like you holding on to things. Use one thing (a door, the monitor, the flashlight, the fan, the heater or the mask) for too long without a break and he jumpscares you and breaks it for a few seconds. He doesn't kill, and he leaves alone whatever has to stay on.
    • 
      
  • Sleepy Moon turns up on one of the cameras every so often, with a soft lullaby. While he's out you drift off and everything gets darker. Find him on the cameras and click him to send him away and wake up. If he's on, he's who comes when the power runs out.
    • 
      
  • Heat: every night your office slowly heats up (the HEAT meter at the top). Turn on the fan now and then to cool it down. If it reaches 100%, you pass out. A heavy breath means it's getting too hot.
    • 
      
  • Fazcoins: every night starts at 0. Coins ($) turn up on the cameras (click them), and you get one each time you clearly fend someone off. Spend them in the SHOP tab: a battery pack (+10% power), Rockstar Bonnie's guitar, or a death coin (15 coins, 3 a night) that takes any one character out for the rest of the night.
    • 
      
  • Crowded nights: the more characters are on, the less often each one acts, so even All 20 can be survived.
    •
